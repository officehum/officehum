# Change checklist

- One booking, one ID. `list_bookings` gives the exact reference; a name is never an ID.
- Check availability before you suggest a move. The calendar is the authority, and a move to the same
  slot is allowed.
- A move or a cancellation waits for a person's approval. Say you have asked, then say what was
  decided.
- Confirm the change with the booking reference and the new time, and say whether the old slot was
  released.
- A request for a discount, a fee waiver or a change outside the usual terms is not yours to grant:
  note it, tell the customer a person will confirm, and finish the change you can make.
