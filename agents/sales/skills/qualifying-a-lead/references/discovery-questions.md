# Discovery questions

Ask these in the client's own language, and stop when you have the answer. Never read the list out.

## Any service

- What is the building, and roughly how big is it — square footage, floors, how many restrooms?
- What happens in it, and who is in it during the day?
- What has to be cleaned or fixed, specifically? What is going wrong now?
- How often, and at what time of day can someone get in?
- Is there a security desk, an alarm, a key, a lift, a loading bay?
- Who signs the contract, and who pays the invoice?
- When do you want this to start, and what has to happen before then?

## Recurring janitorial

- Nightly or weekly? Which nights?
- What is in the current scope that you want kept, and what do you want dropped?
- Who is doing it now, and why are you changing?
- Supplies: are they in the contract, or bought separately?

## Deep clean and floor care

- Has the site been cleaned professionally before, and when was the last deep clean?
- What are the floors — carpet, vinyl, tile, sealed concrete? Any areas that are badly soiled?
- Is the building occupied while the work happens?
- Is it the first clean at this site? (That decides which deep-clean line applies.)

## Windows

- How many panes, roughly, and which floors? (Ground floor is ours; above it is a subcontract.)
- Inside, outside, or both?
- Is there anything in front of the glass that has to be moved?

## Maintenance

- What needs fixing, and is it one job or a list?
- Is it our job to do, or the landlord's? (If it is the landlord's, say so early.)
- Are parts needed, and does the client buy them or do we?

## Day porter and after-hours

- Which hours, which days, and what should the person be doing?
- For a call-out: what has happened, is the building safe, and has anyone been hurt?

## What disqualifies an enquiry

- Outside the Portland service area.
- Residential, construction clean-up, or a service the company does not offer.
- A building nobody can measure or walk, and a client who will not give a size.
- Work above the ground floor on glass or on a roof, without a specialist subcontract.
- A request for a price "today" on a contract nobody has scoped — the answer is a walkthrough, not a
  number.
