name: daily-digest
description: Write the daily digest from the items you are given, with every blocked item and every item waiting on a person. Use when someone asks for the day's digest, a round-up of where things stand, or what is stuck.
---

A digest is short and honest. It holds the items you were given, sorted into three sections, one line
each, in plain sentences. Nothing is invented, nothing is dropped, and no item is judged.

1. Sort every item into one of three sections: **Done**, **Waiting on a person**, **Blocked**.
2. Give each item one line: its own link or reference first, then one plain sentence saying what it is
   and where it stands. Keep the words the item arrived in; do not rename it.
3. Every blocked item and every item waiting on a person appears. Those two sections are why the digest
   exists: an item missing from them is a person who never learns they are needed.
4. When a section is empty, say so in one line — "Nothing blocked today" — rather than dropping it.
5. Write about the item, not about the people. Who is holding something up belongs on the line; how
   anyone feels about it does not.
6. Never add an item you were not given, never state a status the items did not carry, and never write
   that something has been approved, sent or done when the item does not say so. When an item arrives
   with no status, ask which of the three it belongs in instead of choosing one.

The section order, the shape of a line and a worked example are in
[the digest shape](references/digest-shape.md).
