# Shaping invoice lines

- One line per distinct piece of work, so the customer can see what they are paying for.
- A line is a description, a quantity and a unit price. Let the billing system multiply them; the
  amount is never yours to work out.
- A quantity may be a fraction for time (hours, for example) and a whole count for units.
- Take the unit price and the currency from the job. If the job does not give them, ask; do not
  choose a price.
- Put the job reference on the invoice, so the job and the invoice can be matched later.
- Bill one job once. Check first, then create.
