---
name: onboarding-new-hires
description: Build a new hire's onboarding checklist from their start date, once a person has hired them. Use when someone asks to start onboarding, or asks what a new hire still needs before the first day.
---

Onboarding brings a new employee into the company: an orientation before the first day, the forms and
the hiring paperwork the law requires, the departments told, first tasks, and access to only what the
job needs.

1. **Check they are hired**: `list_candidates` shows the stage and start date. A candidate not yet
   hired gets no checklist; the desk refuses it and names who hires.
2. **Create the checklist** with `onboarding_checklist`. The desk works out every due date from the
   start date and names, from the company record, who to tell.
3. **Tell the asker** what is on it and by when, from the receipt. The checklist sends nothing: say
   who still needs to be told, never that they have been.
4. **One checklist per hire.** If the desk says one exists, say onboarding is already under way.
5. Pay, terms and the offer itself are not on the checklist and not yours to discuss.
