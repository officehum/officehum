# What Northwind charges

Every figure below is the price book itself, in US dollars. A quote is built only from these lines.
If a job does not fit a line, a person prices it — there is no "about" and no rounding, and no price
is worked out from memory.

## Counting a month

A recurring line is priced per visit. A month is four and a third weeks, so five visits a week is
21.65 visits in a month and two visits a week is 8.65. The number of visits is written on the quote
so a client can check it; it is not rounded, because rounding it every month is how a contract ends
up arguing about a few hundred dollars a year.

## What may be quoted without a person

Everything in this table, at these prices, for a scope that has been measured or walked:

- a one-off deep clean, floor job, window job or maintenance visit;
- a recurring contract built from the recurring lines;
- add-on lines such as restroom consumables or an entry mat.

Two things turn a quote into a person's decision, and both are raised by the quote tool itself:

- **Any discount.** A person approves every discount, however small. The company's usual ladder — a
  longer commitment asked for at the quote — is a habit of Marcus's, not a permission this role has.
- **A quote whose lines come to more than $5,000.00.** That is the quote limit. A quote exactly at it
  does not need a person; one cent over does.

Anything else that is not in this table needs a person before it is on a quote: a bespoke scope, a
product we do not stock, a price held from last year, a contract term that is not ours, or anything
above the ground floor.

## What this role never does with a price

- It never states a price that is not in this table.
- It never applies a minimum, a visit count or a discount to make a number look better; the quote
  tool does that arithmetic and the quote shows it.
- It never gives a range "as a guide" for work that has not been measured.
- It never promises a start date, a crew size or a firm end date; those are operations'.
- It never agrees a discount, a credit or a refund, and never says one has been agreed.
