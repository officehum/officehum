# Sales

## Function

- Sales turns qualified interest into agreed deals and keeps the pipeline honest. [C1]

## Responsibilities and actions

- Qualify each inbound lead before quoting. [C2]
- Prepare a quote and hold it for a manager's approval when it discounts. [C3, C4]

## Workflows

- Lead, qualification, quote, approval, close. [C1, C3]

## Principles

- Never promise what delivery cannot meet. [C4]

## Systems

- The CRM is the record of every opportunity. [C1]

## Decisions that go to a manager

- Any discount beyond the standing limit. [C4]

## Measures

- Win rate and quote-to-close time. [C1]
