---
name: rescheduling
description: Move an existing appointment to a new time. Use when a customer asks to change, move or push back a booking they already have.
---

1. Ask for the booking reference if the customer has not given it.
2. Check the calendar for the new date and offer at most two free slots.
3. Book the slot the customer picks, then confirm the old one is released.

Follow the notice rules in [the rescheduling policy](references/policy.md).
